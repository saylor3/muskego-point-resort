On the Property

Lodge, store,
and tackle shop.

Everything you need for a day on the water found here. The store keeps live bait, tackle, and gear. Fishing licenses are sold at the counter. Groceries, ice, and snacks are available at the store, and gas is on the property. Boat rentals by the day.

01 - Store

Resort store,
on site

The lodge store carries snacks, ice, lake supplies, and everyday necessities. It's the center of the resort: stop in for the Wi-Fi password, or to ask about the lake, the fishing, or the area.

  • Groceries & snacks
  • Ice
  • Lake supplies
  • Refreshments
  • Gas & oil
  • Laundry
  • Wi-Fi available here
  • Maps & local info

02 - Bait & Tackle

Bait & Tackle
on site

The tackle shop stocks lures, live bait, line, and terminal tackle for every species on Lake Vermilion, including the heavier gear muskie fishing calls for. Fishing licenses are available here.

  • Muskie lures
  • Rapala lures
  • Custom spinnerbaits
  • Soft plastics & jigs
  • Topwater lures
  • Leaders, sinkers, hooks
  • Mono & superline
  • Rods & reels
  • Nets & tackle boxes
  • Live bait
  • Fishing licenses
  • Guide service arranged

03 - Boat Rentals

Boats and dock slips
on site

The resort has aluminum fishing boats and motors available for rent by the day or week. Bring your own boat and keep it in a covered slip right on the dock. Ask when you call for current availability and rates.

See rental rates on the fishing page
