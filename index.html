Why Muskego Point

World-Class Fishing

Lake Vermilion, 40,000 acres and 365 islands, is one of Minnesota's premier muskie and walleye lakes. Muskies up to 55 inches, walleyes past 30.

Private & Unhurried

Each cabin has its own dock on the lake. No crowds, no schedule.

A Century of History

Originally called Goodwill's, the resort has welcomed families to Lake Vermilion for over 100 years.

Built for Families

Cabins sleep 4 to 10 with full kitchens, wood-burning fireplaces, screened porches, and Weber grills.

The Cabins

Cabins

Cabins range from two to five bedrooms, sleeping four to ten. Every cabin has a full kitchen, wood-burning fireplace, screened porch or deck, charcoal grill, and a view of the lake.
