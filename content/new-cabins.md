# New Cabins — Muskego Point Resort

---

## Norway

**Weekly Rate: $2,750**

Norway is a 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom cabin. One bedroom can be configured as 2 twins or 1 king; the other bedroom has a queen bed. The boathouse has been converted into a "dining room" on the water. Includes washer/dryer.

---

## Lumberjack

**Weekly Rate: $3,500**

Lumberjack is a 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom cabin located on the northshore. It includes a separate guest cabin that can be configured as 2 twins or 1 king, with its own bathroom, kitchenette, and washer/dryer. There is also a bunkhouse with a queen bed. In the main cabin, one bedroom has a queen bed and a second bedroom can be configured as 2 twins or 1 king. Features a private sauna, private dock, and shore station.

---

## Spruce

**Weekly Rate: $3,500**

Spruce is a 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom cabin located on the southshore. Three bedrooms can each be configured as 2 twins or 1 king; the fourth bedroom has a queen bed. Features a whirlpool tub, washer/dryer, private sauna, private dock, boat lift, and shared beach.
